Privacy Policy

Arrow Escape 3D

Last Updated: September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Arrow Escape 3D, developed and published by PuzzleSpark Studio.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use our game, including information processed by third-party advertising services.

1. Information We Collect

Arrow Escape 3D does not require users to create an account or directly provide personal information to us.

We do not intentionally collect or store personal information such as the following through our own servers:

  • Name
  • Postal address
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • User account information
  • Payment information
  • Precise location information

However, third-party services used by the game, including advertising services, may collect or process certain information automatically in accordance with their own privacy policies.

2. Local Game Data

The game may store certain gameplay-related information locally on your device, including:

  • Game progress
  • Game settings
  • Game preferences
  • Scores
  • Other gameplay-related information

This gameplay information is stored locally on your device and is not intentionally transmitted to PuzzleSpark Studio's own servers.

3. Advertising

Arrow Escape 3D uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

The game may display different types of advertisements, including:

  • Banner advertisements
  • Rewarded advertisements
  • Interstitial advertisements

Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain information for purposes such as providing, delivering, measuring, and improving advertisements, preventing fraud, and maintaining advertising services.

Depending on applicable laws, device settings, consent status, and advertising configuration, information processed by Google may include identifiers, approximate location information, device or advertising information, and information related to advertising interactions.

Google may use this information to provide personalized or non-personalized advertising as applicable.

For more information about how Google handles information, please review Google's privacy policy:

Google Privacy Policy

4. Internet Permission

Arrow Escape 3D requires internet access for certain features, including advertising services.

Internet access may also be used for future game-related features or updates if such features are introduced.

PuzzleSpark Studio does not intentionally use its own servers to collect or store personal information from users.

5. In-App Purchases

Currently, Arrow Escape 3D does not offer In-App Purchases.

6. User Accounts

Arrow Escape 3D does not currently allow users to create an account.

No login or account registration is required to play the game.
